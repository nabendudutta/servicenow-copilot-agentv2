<h3><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Purpose</span></h3>
<p style="font-size: 11px;" align="left"><span style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">This document includes instructions to remove the Airwatch MDM agent from both Android and iOS devices.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Overview</span></h3>
<h1 style="margin: 12pt 0in 0pt;"><span style="font-family: helvetica;">How to Remove AirWatch</span></h1>
<p style="margin: 1em 0px;"><span style="font-family: helvetica;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">These instructions are offered as general instructions for all devices. Your device’s actual steps may vary. If you have questions or need assistance with your mobile phone, please contact IT.</span></span></p>
<p style="margin: 1em 0px;"><span style="font-family: helvetica;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">Access to email remotely will no longer require AirWatch be installed on your device.</span></span></p>
<h2 style="margin: 2pt 0in 0pt;"><span style="font-family: helvetica;">How to uninstall the AirWatch software from an Android Device</span></h2>
<ol style="list-style-position: inside;"><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Settings</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Security &gt; Device administrators.</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Agent to uncheck the item.</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Deactivate and then touch OK.</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Go to the Home screen.</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Uninstall.</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Settings</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Application Manager &gt; Agent, and then touch Uninstall twice.</li></ol>
<h2 style="margin: 2pt 0in 0pt;"><span style="font-family: helvetica;">How to uninstall the AirWatch software from an iOS Device</span></h2>
<ol style="list-style-position: inside;"><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch Settings</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Touch General</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Scroll the Bottom</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Select the Option “Device Management” or “Profiles”</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Select “AirWatch MDM/V_?”</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Tap the Red “Remove Management” button</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Enter your normal phone Passcode</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Tap the Red “Remove” button</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Return to the main phone screen</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Tap and hold the AirWatch Agent Application</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Wait until it shakes</li><li style="font-family: helvetica; font-size: 14pt;">Tap the small ‘x’ in the upper right hand corner</li></ol>
